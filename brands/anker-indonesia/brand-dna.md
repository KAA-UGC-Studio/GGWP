# Anker Indonesia — Brand DNA

## Identity
**Name:** Anker Indonesia (parent brand written as "Anker"; sub-brands Soundcore, Eufy, Nebula, AnkerWork)
**Positioning:** The world's No.1 mobile charging brand for Indonesian consumers — fast, reliable power and audio tech that keeps mobile life truly mobile.
**Voice:** Confident, energetic, tech-precise, aspirational, approachable
**Target audience:** Tech-savvy Indonesian professionals and digitally-native younger users (20–40) who carry multiple devices and value speed, portability, and proven reliability.

## Color System
Lead:        Anker Cyan Blue — #40C7FB (estimated from brand palette sources)
Support:     Cod Gray (near-black) — #111111
Accent:      White — #FFFFFF (used as reversed type on blue/black)
Background:  Clean White — #FFFFFF, with dark charcoal (#111111) for premium "Prime" lines
Off-limits:  Warm earth tones, pastels, decorative gradients unrelated to the cyan-blue energy palette

## Typography
Headline:    Bold, modern geometric sans-serif (matches the Anker wordmark style — exact corporate face not published)
Body:        Clean regular-weight sans-serif, generous spacing
Treatment:   Sentence case or Title Case headlines; benefit-led lines kept short ("Power to Drive Your Prime"); wattage and specs (45W, 65W, GaN) rendered large as visual proof points
In ads:      Minimal copy on image — one short headline plus a spec callout, white or cyan type over dark or clean product backgrounds

## Photography Style
- **Lighting:** Controlled studio lighting — crisp, even, slightly directional to model the product's edges and finish; no harsh shadows
- **What's in frame:** The product as hero — chargers, power banks, cables, earbuds shot solo or with the devices they power (phone, laptop); occasional hands for scale, rarely full lifestyle models
- **Color grade:** Cool and clean, punchy contrast, true blacks and whites; cyan-blue accents echo the brand color
- **Setting:** Seamless studio backgrounds (white, light gray, or dark charcoal); occasionally scenic/contextual backdrops for travel-oriented products
- **Composition:** Head-on or slight three-quarter angles, product centered with generous negative space; tight macro details for ports and textures
- **Mood:** Precise, powerful, modern, trustworthy, effortless

## The Product
Form:       Compact consumer electronics — matte-finish chargers and power banks (white or black plastic/GaN housings), braided and silicone cables, TWS earbud cases, speakers, docking stations
Label:      "Anker" wordmark in bold sans-serif printed small on the housing; series names (Nano, Prime, PowerLine, GaNPrime) plus wattage ratings feature prominently on packaging

## Ad Creative Style
Formats:      Feed square and Stories on Instagram/Facebook; short-form video for launches; product-led carousel for line-ups
Text on image: Minimal — one benefit headline plus a wattage/spec callout; heavy copy avoided
Visual style:  Product-only hero shots dominate, high production quality; occasional CGI/AR spectacle for launch campaigns (e.g., 2025 Southeast Asia landmark campaign)
Current ads:   Meta Ad Library could not be read directly; Instagram (@ankerindonesia, 104K followers) runs product-hero posts and promos under the "World's No.1 Mobile Charging Brand" banner

## Voice in Practice
Sounds like: "Professional fast charging, all in a Nano — 45W of power that fits in your pocket."
Never:       "Buy this cheap charger now, big discount, don't miss out!!!"

## Hard Rules
Always: Product is the hero — sharp, well-lit, centered, with clean negative space
Always: Cyan-blue #40C7FB present as the energy accent (logo, type, or light detail)
Never:  Cluttered lifestyle scenes that hide the product or bury it in props
Never:  Warm vintage filters, pastel palettes, or handwritten/script typography
