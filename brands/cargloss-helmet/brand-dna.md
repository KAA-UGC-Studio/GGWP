# Cargloss Helmet — Brand DNA

## Identity
**Name:** Cargloss Helmet (wordmark set in caps as "CARGLOSS", racing lockup "CARGLOSS RACING")
**Positioning:** Indonesian helmet maker that treats a helmet as part of your lifestyle, not just a legal box to tick — premium head protection made accessible to every rider, where safety and style carry equal weight.
**Voice:** Confident, style-forward, protective, grounded, community-minded
**Target audience:** Indonesian urban riders and daily commuters — style-conscious men and women (including a large hijab-wearing segment) who want an affordable helmet that looks as good as it protects.

## Color System
Lead:        Deep Black — #1A1A1A
Support:     Clean White — #F5F4F1
Accent:      Racing Red — #D22B2B
Background:  Warm Studio Grey — #E9E6E1
Off-limits:  Neon / high-saturation gamer colors, cluttered gradients — colorways stay muted and tonal (pale pink, sage green, lilac, mocca, avorio cream)

## Typography
Headline:    Bold condensed sans-serif, uppercase (motorsport/utility feel)
Body:        Clean humanist sans-serif, regular weight
Treatment:   Category and headline text set ALL-CAPS with wide tracking; product names in sentence case with descriptive colorway qualifiers ("CFM Retro Deep Black", "Avorio Naiade")
In ads:      Type sits minimal — short uppercase headline or the "RIDE IN STYLE" line, usually cornered or baseline over lifestyle/product imagery, never crowding the helmet

## Photography Style
- **Lighting:** Soft, even studio light for product shots — clean highlights that read the paint finish (gloss vs. doff/matte); natural daylight for lifestyle riding shots
- **What's in frame:** The helmet as hero, shot from consistent multi-angle views (front, three-quarter "serong", side, back, interior detail); in lifestyle frames, riders on motorbikes in urban/street settings
- **Color grade:** Neutral to slightly warm, clean and true-to-color so muted pastel colorways stay accurate; moderate contrast, not filmic
- **Setting:** Seamless neutral studio backdrop (white / warm grey) for catalog; real Indonesian street and urban-commuter environments for lifestyle
- **Composition:** Helmet centered with generous negative space; tight enough to show finish and shell detail, angled three-quarter as the signature product view
- **Mood:** Clean, aspirational, effortless, everyday-cool

## The Product
Form:       ABS half-face and full-face motorcycle helmets — retro round-shell (CFM/CFS), sport half-face (NFS), hijab-integrated (YRH/CFH), plus flat "pet" visors and peaks; matte (doff) and gloss finishes
Label:      Cargloss wordmark on the shell; SNI / SNI-standard certification marking; colorway name on packaging and listing

## Ad Creative Style
Formats:      Instagram-led (feed square, Reels/Stories), product carousels of colorways, lifestyle riding video
Text on image: Sparing — short uppercase headline, price/promo bar ("FREE SHIPPING ON ORDERS ABOVE IDR 500.000"), or the "RIDE IN STYLE" tagline
Visual style:  Mixed — clean studio product shots of each colorway paired with real-rider lifestyle content
Current ads:   Colorway-focused product and lifestyle posts across a 140K-follower Instagram (@carglosshelmets), spotlighting retro CFM and NFS lines and new colorways

## Voice in Practice
Sounds like: "Wear your helmet, start your engine, and let's explore safely. Ride in style."
Never:       "The ultimate extreme adrenaline machine — dominate the streets!!!"

## Hard Rules
Always: Helmet is the hero, shot clean at a three-quarter angle so shell shape and paint finish read clearly
Always: Keep colorways true-to-color and tonal — muted pastels and clean neutrals, never oversaturated
Never:  Bury the helmet under heavy text, busy gradients, or neon graphics
Never:  Make the brand feel cheap or reckless — safety and considered style always come through
