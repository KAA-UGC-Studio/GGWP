# SKIN1004 — Brand DNA

## Identity
**Name:** SKIN1004 (all caps, no space; product lines lead with "Madagascar Centella")
**Positioning:** A Korean skincare brand for sensitive, reactive skin. It uses Centella Asiatica sourced from Madagascar in lightweight, gently effective formulas and comes across as calm, pure, and quietly scientific.
**Voice:** Serene, nature-reverent, gently clinical, sincere, understated
**Target audience:** K-beauty shoppers aged 18–35 with sensitive, redness-prone, or congested skin. They want calm, simple routines with clean ingredients, and they respond to proof-backed claims and good value.

## Color System
Lead:        Centella Gold — #A8915F
Support:     Earth Bronze — #7D6C48
Accent:      Deep Umber — #674E26
Background:  Pure White — #FFFFFF
Off-limits:  Neon or saturated brights, harsh primary red, pure-black grounds on the core Centella line, glossy chrome/metallic effects, and cold inical blue-white lab lighting

Line-coded secondary tints (used per product line on packaging and in campaigns): Hyalu-Cica dusty blue, Poremizing blush pink, Tone Brightening soft white/peach, Tea-Trica green, Probio-Cica lilac. Warm cream set backgrounds sit around #EDE6DA (estimated).

## Typography
Headline:    Elegant high-contrast serif in all caps for campaign heroes (e.g. "CENTELLA", "HYALU-"), paired with a flowing handwritten script for sub-line names ("Teca"). On pack it's a bold geometric sans, all caps ("CENTELLA").
Body:        Assistant, regular (clean humanist sans on site and in body copy)
Treatment:   Very wide-tracked tiny caps as eyebrow lines ("M A D A G A S C A R") sitting above a heavier line name. Strong scale contrast between a small eyebrow and a large name. Serif caps mixed with a script accent. The wordmark "SKIN1004" is thin and widely letter-spaced.
In ads:      Brand-direct heroes keep type small and left-aligned over soft negative space, in white or deep umber on image. Regional performance ads use bold sale numerals (50% / 10%), pill badges, and benefit callouts stacked beside the product.

## Photography Style
- **Lighting:** Two looks. Portraits use bright, soft, diffused studio light on white seamless with almost no shadow. Still lifes use warm, low-angle golden light with long soft shadows and a gentle glow through the amber liquid.
- **What's in frame:** Models (men and women, East Asian leads) hold the bottle right against the cheek or jaw, with direct eye contact, bare natural skin, and off-white knit or linen clothing. Still lifes use natural props: smooth stones, pebbles, carved wood, raw bark, ceramic pedestals, and sculptural ribbon forms. Products are grouped by line in 2–4 pieces.
- **Color grade:** Warm and gently filmic on the Centella and Teca lines (golden, honeyed, low contrast, matte). Clean and neutral on portraits. Cooler dusty blue-grey on Hyalu lines. Skin keeps real texture.
- **Setting:** White seamless studio; warm cream or gold gradient backdrops; wood and sand surfaces; open outdoor scenes (grass fields, big sky, Madagascar-style golden sunset).
- **Composition:** Products are centred or right-weighted with generous negative space on the left for type. Portraits are tight head-and-shoulders at eye level with the label facing camera. Product shots are front-on or at a slight low angle, with bottles staggered in height.
- **Mood:** Serene, grounded, pure, warm, quietly premium

## The Product
Form:       Clear cylindrical bottles showing amber-gold liquid, with white rubber-bulb dropper or pump caps; soft-matte squeeze tubes and squat jars in line-coded tints
Label:      Centered white rectangular panel. Tiny wide-tracked "MADAGASCAR" sits above a bold sans "CENTELLA", then the product name in small caps, then "MADE WITH PURE CENTELLA FROM MADAGASCAR" and the volume in fine print. The thin, spaced "SKIN1004" wordmark sits at the bottom of the panel.

## Ad Creative Style
Formats:      Feed square and 4:5 images, Reels/Stories vertical video, and Shopee-linked catalog/carousel ads
Text on image: Brand ads use a headline plus the line name, placed sparingly. Performance ads carry heavier copy: sale badges, benefit bullets, and exact proof numbers (92.69% microdust removed, 39.44% fewer dark spots, −6.31°C skin cooling).
Visual style:  Mixed. Model-holding-product close-ups, clean product still lifes, and UGC-style texture and before/after video.
Current ads:   SKIN1004_Indonesia and SKIN1004 Malaysia are running dozens of active ads, mostly in Bahasa. They focus on sunscreens, Poremizing, Centella Soothing Cream, and Tone Brightening, use problem–solution hooks with emoji bullets, and push to Shopee Mall.

## Voice in Practice
Sounds like: "Pure Centella from Madagascar, refined just enough to calm what your skin has been carrying all day."
Never:       "OMG this serum is a total GAME-CHANGER 🔥🔥 your skin will be SNATCHED overnight!!!"

## Hard Rules
Always: Show the white label panel facing camera with "MADAGASCAR CENTELLA" legible, and keep the ampoule liquid a true amber-gold.
Always: Use soft, natural-feeling light (bright diffused white or warm golden hour) with real, unretouched skin texture and calm expressions.
Never:  Use neon, saturated, or clashing colors, or put a product on a background from another line's color code.
Never:  Clutter the frame, use plastic or synthetic props, or use glossy chrome styling. Props stay natural (stone, wood, ceramic, botanicals).
