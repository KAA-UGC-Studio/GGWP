# Poremizing Light Gel Cream — Product DNA

## Identity
**Category:** Skincare — lightweight gel moisturizer (pore care)
**Sizes / flavors / scents:** 75ml / 2.53 fl oz tube

## What it does
**Tagline:** Lightweight Cooling Gel Cream
**Description:** A watery gel cream from the Madagascar Centella Poremizing line that cools on contact, hydrates for hours, and refines the look of pores with a smooth, dewy finish. Made for oily, combination and sensitive skin that wants moisture without heaviness.

## Key benefits
- Instant refreshing, cooling sensation with lasting hydration — watery gel texture, smooth dewy finish
- Refines the appearance of pores — Himalayan pink salt helps purify; aloe vera and adenosine help improve the look of firmness and pores
- Helps even out tone for a brighter-looking complexion with niacinamide
- Soothes and comforts with Madagascar Centella, cypress tree water and witch hazel
- Mild exfoliating pore care, gentle enough to use as the last step of a daily routine

## Ingredients / materials
- Madagascar Centella Asiatica Extract
- Mineral Salts (Himalayan pink salt)
- Aloe Barbadensis (Aloe Vera) Leaf Extract
- Niacinamide
- Adenosine
- Hamamelis Virginiana (Witch Hazel) Leaf Water
- Chamaecyparis Obtusa (Cypress Tree) Water
- Hyaluronic acid complex (Hyaluronic Acid, Hydrolyzed Hyaluronic Acid, Sodium Hyaluronate), Panthenol, Kaolin
- Notable absences:

## Positioning
The pore-care moisturizer of the Madagascar Centella range: lighter and cooler than Centella Cream, and aimed at pores where Centella Soothing Cream targets redness. Pink salt pore care in a gel-cream texture means oily skin doesn't have to skip moisturizer.
