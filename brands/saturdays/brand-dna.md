# SATURDAYS — Brand DNA

## Identity
**Name:** SATURDAYS (always all-caps)
**Positioning:** Indonesia's first and largest tech-enabled eyewear brand — in-house-designed acetate and titanium frames that turn premium eyewear into an everyday, weekend-lifestyle choice for fashion-conscious young Indonesians; comes across playful, accessible, and self-assured.
**Voice:** Playful, breezy, confident, inclusive, tech-forward
**Target audience:** Style-conscious, digitally-native Indonesian millennials and Gen Z (roughly 18–35) in urban centres who treat eyewear as an everyday lifestyle accessory and form of self-expression.

## Color System
Lead:        Black — #111111
Support:     White — #FFFFFF
Accent:      Vibrant Yellow — #FFD400  (estimated — campaign brights; easy to correct)
Background:  Soft off-white — #F5F5F3
Off-limits:  Dark, moody, grungy palettes; heavy gradients; muddy or desaturated tones

## Typography
Headline:    Bold grotesque sans-serif, all-caps (matches the wordmark)
Body:        Clean humanist sans-serif, regular
Treatment:   All-caps headlines set tight and confident; generous whitespace; minimal, uncluttered hierarchy
In ads:      Type sits sparingly — a short punchy headline or offer; the product and face carry the frame, not the copy

## Photography Style
- **Lighting:** Bright, soft and even — clean studio light on product; natural daylight for lifestyle. Nothing harsh or dramatic.
- **What's in frame:** The frames themselves (crisp product shots) and young, diverse Indonesian faces wearing them — close crops on the eyes, relaxed head-and-shoulders portraits.
- **Color grade:** Clean and true-to-life — bright, high clarity, a touch warm; never filmic or heavily filtered.
- **Setting:** White or soft-neutral seamless for product; real urban weekend settings — cafés, streets, outdoors — for lifestyle.
- **Composition:** Product centred on white with generous negative space; candid, easygoing framing for lifestyle portraits.
- **Mood:** Fresh, easygoing, optimistic, youthful, polished

## The Product
Form:       Optical glasses and sunglasses — slim modern silhouettes in Italian cellulose acetate and Japanese titanium
Label:      Frames named with human first names (model names); SATURDAYS wordmark on the temple arm

## Ad Creative Style
Formats:      Feed square + Stories/Reels, short video (AR / home try-on), product carousels — strongly app-led
Text on image: Used sparingly — short headline or promo offer; imagery does the talking
Visual style:  Mixed — clean product-on-white alternating with lifestyle imagery of young Indonesians
Current ads:   Live Meta Ad Library couldn't be read automatically; based on their channels they typically run new-collection launches, home-try-on prompts, and seasonal offers across lifestyle + product creative.

## Voice in Practice
Sounds like: "Eyewear shopping, easy peasy lemon squeezy. Find your everyday pair and make it yours — style powered by you."
Never:       "Precision-engineered optical instruments for the discerning connoisseur."

## Hard Rules
Always: Keep it clean and bright — generous white space, product in sharp focus
Always: Feature real, diverse young Indonesian faces in lifestyle shots
Never:  Dark, moody, grungy or heavily filtered treatments
Never:  Cluttered layouts or heavy text stacked over the image
